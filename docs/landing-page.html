Questions couples will ask first.

Clear answers help the app feel safe, practical, and worth installing.

Does the app need to stay open?

No. Widgets refresh from locally cached data after push/background updates. The app is used for setup and richer actions.

Is location exact?

Distance should be approximate by default. Users can choose whether to share it at all.

Is this a dating app?

No. Connect is for couples who are already together and want a private shared space.

Will it work on Android?

Android is the best MVP target because widgets and home-screen interactions are more flexible.
